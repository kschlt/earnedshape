# Licensing

Two licences, split by what the material is.

| Path | Licence | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `tools/**` and any future code | **MIT** (`LICENSE`) | permissive, no friction for anyone integrating a check into their own pipeline |
| `protocol/**`, `capabilities/**`, `research/**`, `docs/**`, `README.md` | **CC BY 4.0** (`LICENSE-DOCS`) | the written specification is the substance; attribution keeps a claim traceable to the version that made it |

If a file's classification is ever unclear, the rule is: executable is MIT, prose is CC BY 4.0.

## Why attribution matters more than usual here

This project's claims are attached to specific versions of specific rules, and most of them are unproven. An adaptation that changes a rule and keeps the name would make the evidence unreadable — not because of ownership, but because nobody could tell which protocol a result belonged to. Adaptations are welcome; adaptations that say so are what keep the evidence usable.
